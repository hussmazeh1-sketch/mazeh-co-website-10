01 — Mazeh Co.

Material knowledge.

Mazeh Co. is a natural-stone manufacturing and project-execution company operating in Tyre, Lebanon and Valencia, Spain.

We combine industrial stone production with project-led fabrication: cutting blocks into slabs, refining surfaces, translating dimensions and drawings, and producing bespoke elements for architectural settings.

From raw stone
to exact detail.

A project-oriented manufacturing workflow combining slab preparation, surface finishing, dimensional fabrication and CNC waterjet work.

Slab finishing

06 — Production & technology

CNC waterjet.
Drawings become stone.

CNC waterjet cutting translates approved digital geometry into custom stone components. It is especially useful where the design calls for curves, inlays, complex profiles, repeated shapes or decorative work.

Conceptual CNC waterjet workflow

  1. 01
    Digital drawingApproved profiles and geometry establish the cut path.
  2. 02
    Material planningPiece orientation and layout are considered before fabrication.
  3. 03
    CNC waterjet cuttingComputer-controlled cutting follows the programmed geometry without claiming a specific machine brand or capacity.
  4. 04
    Detail & finishingCut pieces can move into edge work, finishing and other project-specific fabrication stages.
  5. 05
    Quality reviewComponents are checked against the approved fabrication scope before delivery or installation preparation.
