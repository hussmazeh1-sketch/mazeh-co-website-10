WEBSITE 10 — ADDING IMAGES

1. Open the content-images folder.
2. Put your approved JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP or AVIF image into the matching category folder.
3. Do NOT edit HTML to add the image.
4. For local preview, run: python build_gallery.py
5. Netlify runs build_gallery.py automatically on deployment when netlify.toml is used.
6. Images are displayed with the website's responsive gallery sizing automatically. Original files are not distorted.

Examples:
content-images/projects/kitchens-countertops/kitchen-01.jpg
content-images/projects/swimming-pools/pool-01.webp
content-images/finishes/leathered/leathered-01.jpg

The mapping of every page to every folder is stored in gallery-config.json.
